What it does

AI coach, on device

Picks today's session from muscle readiness, your split and training days, using Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. A built-in coach takes over when it's off.

Learns your limits

Tell it about an injury or a lift you can't do yet, and it plans around it.

Equipment-aware

Gym profiles for home, commercial and travel. The coach only programs what that gym has.

Finds your weights

Test sets find a starting weight for each lift, then progressive overload moves it up as you get stronger.

Plans the week

Every training day gets a plan. Move, shorten or skip any of them.

Form guides

A page for every exercise, plus a warm-up and cool-down built into each workout.

Apple Watch and Health

Log sets on your wrist, see the rest timer on the Lock Screen, and find every workout in Fitness.

Crews (beta)

Weekly streaks with friends over iCloud sharing. Crews only see check-ins.

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Privacy policy
