The coach

An AI coach that never leaves your iPhone.

Built on Apple Intelligence and run entirely on device. Tell it what's going on, in your own words, and today's plan changes to match.

  • A new plan every dayBuilt from how recovered each muscle is, your schedule, the equipment at your gym and how the last session felt.
  • The rules pick the weightsEvery number comes from your own training history. The model only puts it into words.
  • It remembers what you tell itCan't do a pull-up yet? It saves the note, plans around it and offers to build you up to one, step by step.

The workout

Built for the middle of a set.

Big targets, one tap to log, and no forms to fill in while you catch your breath.

01

Start warm.

A short warm-up is built into every workout, and heavy lifts get lighter ramp-up sets you can edit. Or skip straight to lifting.

02

Tap the ring. Set logged.

Your target weight and reps are already filled in. Tap a set's ring to log it, and tap again to undo.

03

A keypad made for plates.

Change the weight with quick steps instead of typing it out.

−5−2.5+2.5+5
04

Rest on your terms.

The rest timer starts itself and shows what's next. Take 30 seconds off, add 30, or skip it.

−30Skip rest+30
05

Rate each lift once.

One tap after the last set. That answer sets the weights for your next session.

Too easyJust rightToo hard
06

Tuck it away.

Minimize the workout to a bar and look around the app. It keeps running, one tap away.

07

Finish strong.

Finish for a summary with your personal bests, volume and heart rate. Changed your mind? Discard the workout and nothing is saved.
